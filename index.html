What this model is and is not

Each lineage follows a stem cell → progenitor → three maturation stages → blood chain with feedback from the circulating count, after the Friberg myelosuppression model. Radiation kills dividing marrow cells and circulating lymphocytes by the linear-quadratic model with repair of sublethal damage, so fractionation and low dose rate are less damaging than the same dose given at once. Shielded marrow is simulated as a separate site that keeps producing cells.

It does not model mortality, infection, bleeding, transfusion, gastrointestinal or cutaneous injury, the early neutrophil rise from demargination, or long-term stem-cell exhaustion and clonal effects. Space-radiation quality (high-LET ions) is not represented beyond absorbed dose. Treat outputs as hypotheses for experiments, not clinical guidance.
