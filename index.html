About this simulation

The animation is driven by a mathematical model of blood production: stem cells feed growing cells, which mature over days and enter the blood, and the marrow speeds up when counts are low. Radiation kills dividing cells, but cells repair some damage when the dose is spread out. Each dot on screen stands for a share of the real population, so fewer dots means fewer cells.

This is a research and teaching tool, not medical advice. It leaves out infection, bleeding, transfusions, injury to other organs, and the special effects of heavy particles in space radiation. It is a first step toward a personal “digital twin” that would be tuned with each person’s own marrow cells grown on a tissue chip.
